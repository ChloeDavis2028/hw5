What I learned. The agents are good at routing, but the money has to be protected by code. On every ticket the Boss made the right first call: Inventory for Tauhid's tee (101), Facilities for the rent (102) and Inventory for the AI Club hoodies (103). Still, nothing on the Cash tab moved until a person signed in and approved it. Chloe and Pat both had to approve the $2,400.00 rent, and Chloe approved invoice 501 ($840.00). Checking went from $3,400.00 to $160.00 and matched cash_accounts exactly. I trust that number because a script checks it, not because an agent said so.

What I'd do differently. I'd give each agent the facts it needs up front. Customer Service re-ran lookups that Inventory and Facilities had already done: 6 extra on 101 and 2 extra on 102. On 103 it even tried to hand the reply to itself. The Flight Instructor caught it, I fixed one prompt, and 102 went from B to A. I'd also write my prompts in one message with the port, the rules and "done means" first. Most of my rework came from details I added late.

What surprised me. The tickets were "resolved," but the shop isn't healthy. After paying the rent and Bulldog Print Co, Campus Customs has $160.00, below its own $500 cushion. So it can't restock the 12 hoodies the Yale AI Club is short until the $522 deposit comes in. A finished board doesn't mean a healthy business. The next thing I'd build is a way to track money coming in.
